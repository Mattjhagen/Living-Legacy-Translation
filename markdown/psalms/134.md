# Psalms 134

*Rooted Daily Global Bible — Old Testament*

[← Psalms 133](./133.md) | [Psalms](./README.md) | [Psalms 135 →](./135.md)

---

**1** <b>A Song of Ascents. </b>   Look! Praise Yahweh, all you servants of Yahweh, who stand by night in Yahweh’s house! 

**2** Lift up your hands in the sanctuary. Praise Yahweh! 

**3** May Yahweh bless you from Zion; even he who made heaven and earth.
