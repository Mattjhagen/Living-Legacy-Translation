Hosea 1

The Promiscuous Wife and the Children of Unfaithfulness.

Go, Marry a Promiscuous Woman

“For like an adulterous wife this land is guilty of unfaithfulness to the Lord.” 1

The Firstborn: Jezreel

“Call him Jezreel, because I will soon punish the house of Jehu.” 2

Lo-Ruhamah & Lo-Ammi

“Lo-Ruhamah: Not Loved.”
“Lo-Ammi: You are not my people, and I am not your God.” 3

Children of the Living God

“They will be called ‘children of the living God.’” 4
“For great will be the day of Jezreel.” 5

Translation Notes

1 Promiscuous Woman (ē-sheth ze-nū-nīm): Literally "Woman of whorings." The scrolls highlight the "Unfaithful Root of the Land." "Covenantal Love" is "Uprooted" by "Adultery" (*Zanah*). It signifies "The Aesthetic of Sacred Betrayal."

2 Jezreel (Yiz-re-el): Literally "God Sows." The scrolls highlight the "Agricultural Root of Judgment." "Peace" is "Uprooted" in the "Valley of Jezreel." God "Roots" his "Punishment" in the "Name." It signify "The Sowing of Destruction."

3 Lo-Ammi (Lō Am-mī): The scrolls highlight the "Disowned Root of the Covenant." "Belonging" is "Uprooted." God "Roots" the "Separation" in the "Negation" (*Lo*). It signifies "The Ultimate Rejection of the Unfaithful."

4 Children of the Living God (be-nē ēl-chāy): The scrolls highlight the "Adoptive Root of Grace." "Orphanhood" is "Uprooted." The "People" are "Re-Rooted" as "Children" (*Banim*) of the "Living God." It signify "The Reversal of the Curse."

5 Day of Jezreel (yōm Yiz-re-el): The scrolls conclude with this "Sovereign Root of Restoration." "God Sows" is transformed from "Judgment" to "Reunion." It marks the transition to "Israel’s Punishment and Restoration" in *Hosea 2*. It is the "Rooted Daily" anthem of "The Children of the Living God." You are mine again.
